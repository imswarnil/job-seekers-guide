# The book brief

## What this folder is

The book currently runs about 6,000 words across 16 chapters. To reach the
~100 printed pages you asked for it needs roughly 14,000 — and the missing
8,000 are **facts only you have**. I can pace a story, break chapters and set
type. I cannot invent what Kota smelled like at 6am or what your sister
actually said, and a story about a real person that has been filled in with
plausible-sounding invention is worth less than a short honest one.

So: one file per chapter, each with a handful of questions. Answer in whatever
form you like — bullet points, voice-note transcripts, half sentences, Hindi
where English will not do. Rough is fine. Wrong grammar is fine. **Detail is the
only thing that matters.** I will do the writing.

## How to answer

Under each question, just type. Do not tidy it up. If a question does not apply,
write `skip` and move on. If something occurs to you that is not asked, put it
under **Anything else** at the bottom — those are usually the best bits.

## What makes an answer useful

The difference between 200 words and 900 words in a chapter is almost always
one of these five things, so the questions keep asking for them:

1. **A number.** Not "cheap rent" — "₹2,800 a month, shared with two others."
2. **A thing you can see.** The colour of the walls. What was on the desk. What
   you were wearing. What the room sounded like.
3. **Something somebody said, in their words.** Even approximately. Dialogue is
   the single highest-value thing you can give me.
4. **What you believed at the time** — including the things you now know were
   wrong. The reader is currently believing those same things. That is the
   whole point of the book.
5. **What you did that day.** Not the summary. The actual hours.

## Where the answers go

I read this folder and rewrite `content/6.story/*.md` from it. Nothing here is
published — this folder is excluded from the build. You can leave it messy
forever.
