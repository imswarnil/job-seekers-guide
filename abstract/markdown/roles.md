# Role Reference Data

Used by `CS-000` Part 4 ("The roles, honestly") and by the target-role selector
in [onboarding step 4](../flows/04-goal-and-destination.md).

Every role card answers the same five questions, because those are the questions
a beginner actually has and rarely gets answered.

> **Salary figures are indicative ranges for India, and are dummy placeholder
> data in this seed file.** They must be reviewed against current market data
> before publishing, and dated when they are. Nothing here should be presented
> to a learner as a promise.

---

## Backend Developer
- **On a Tuesday:** writing APIs, fixing a bug from yesterday's release,
  reviewing a teammate's pull request, arguing about a database schema.
- **Foundations it leans on:** DBMS ▲▲▲ · OS ▲▲ · Networks ▲▲ · OOP ▲▲▲ ·
  System Design ▲▲▲
- **Language:** Java, Python, or TypeScript/Node
- **Entry difficulty:** medium · **Fresher hiring volume:** very high
- **Indicative range:** ₹3–6 LPA fresher · ₹12–25 LPA at 5 yrs

## Frontend Developer
- **On a Tuesday:** building a screen from a design, making it work on mobile,
  chasing a state bug, talking to the designer.
- **Foundations:** Web Tech ▲▲▲ · OOP ▲▲ · Networks ▲ · DSA ▲
- **Language:** TypeScript/JavaScript
- **Entry difficulty:** low-medium · **Volume:** high
- **Indicative range:** ₹3–6 LPA fresher · ₹12–22 LPA at 5 yrs

## Full-Stack Developer
- **On a Tuesday:** all of the above, on a smaller team, with less specialisation
  and more ownership.
- **Foundations:** everything, moderately
- **Entry difficulty:** medium · **Volume:** high, especially at startups
- **Indicative range:** ₹3.5–7 LPA fresher · ₹15–28 LPA at 5 yrs

## Data Engineer
- **On a Tuesday:** moving data between systems, fixing a pipeline that failed
  at 3am, optimising a slow query, modelling a warehouse table.
- **Foundations:** DBMS ▲▲▲ · OS ▲▲ · Networks ▲▲ · System Design ▲▲▲ · DSA ▲
- **Language:** Python + SQL
- **Entry difficulty:** medium-high · **Volume:** medium, growing
- **Indicative range:** ₹4–8 LPA fresher · ₹18–35 LPA at 5 yrs

## Data Analyst
- **On a Tuesday:** answering a business question with SQL, building a dashboard,
  presenting findings to people who won't read a notebook.
- **Foundations:** DBMS ▲▲▲ · statistics ▲▲ · communication ▲▲▲
- **Language:** SQL + Python
- **Entry difficulty:** low-medium · **Volume:** medium
- **Indicative range:** ₹3.5–6 LPA fresher · ₹10–20 LPA at 5 yrs

## QA / Test Automation Engineer
- **On a Tuesday:** writing automated tests, reproducing a bug someone swears
  doesn't exist, reviewing a release, maintaining a test framework.
- **Foundations:** SE ▲▲▲ · OOP ▲▲ · Web Tech ▲▲ · Networks ▲▲
- **Language:** Java or Python
- **Entry difficulty:** low · **Volume:** high — **a genuinely underrated entry
  door**, and easy to move into development from
- **Indicative range:** ₹3–5 LPA fresher · ₹10–20 LPA at 5 yrs

## DevOps / Cloud Engineer
- **On a Tuesday:** a broken deploy pipeline, a cloud bill nobody understands,
  writing infrastructure scripts, being on call.
- **Foundations:** OS ▲▲▲ · Networks ▲▲▲ · SE ▲▲ · System Design ▲▲
- **Language:** Python/Bash + YAML everywhere
- **Entry difficulty:** high for a true fresher · **Volume:** medium
- **Indicative range:** ₹4–7 LPA fresher · ₹18–35 LPA at 5 yrs
- **Note:** often entered *after* 1–2 years in development or support

## Mobile Developer
- **On a Tuesday:** building a screen, fighting the platform, handling offline
  state, preparing a store release.
- **Foundations:** OOP ▲▲▲ · Networks ▲▲ · DBMS ▲▲
- **Language:** Java/Kotlin (Android) or TypeScript (React Native)
- **Entry difficulty:** medium · **Volume:** medium
- **Indicative range:** ₹3.5–6 LPA fresher · ₹14–25 LPA at 5 yrs

## Salesforce Developer / Admin
- **On a Tuesday:** configuring an org for a client, writing Apex for something
  configuration can't do, meeting business users, running a data migration.
- **Foundations:** DBMS ▲▲ · OOP ▲▲ · SE ▲▲ · communication ▲▲▲
- **Language:** Apex (Java-like) + declarative tooling
- **Entry difficulty:** low-medium · **Volume:** steady, less competition
- **Indicative range:** ₹3.5–6 LPA fresher · ₹15–30 LPA at 5 yrs
- **Note:** this is the track that took me into Accenture in 2017. Dismissed
  online as "not real coding"; pays well, hires freshers steadily, and has a far
  thinner applicant pool than web development.

## Business Analyst / Product
- **On a Tuesday:** talking to users, writing requirements, arguing about
  priorities, translating between business and engineering.
- **Foundations:** SE ▲▲▲ · DBMS ▲▲ · communication ▲▲▲▲
- **Entry difficulty:** medium — **often the best fit for career changers with
  domain expertise**
- **Indicative range:** ₹4–7 LPA fresher · ₹15–30 LPA at 5 yrs

## Support / Implementation Engineer
- **On a Tuesday:** a customer's broken setup, reproducing an issue, escalating
  a real bug, writing documentation.
- **Foundations:** Networks ▲▲ · DBMS ▲▲ · communication ▲▲▲
- **Entry difficulty:** low — **the most accessible door into IT**
- **Indicative range:** ₹2.5–4.5 LPA fresher
- **Note:** a legitimate entry point, and a bad place to stay for four years. If
  you take this, plan the move out from month one — see
  [../curriculum/09-offer-and-switch.md](../curriculum/09-offer-and-switch.md).

## The shared floor

The point of showing all eleven side by side is what they have in common:

> Every single one stands on **operating systems, databases, networks, how code
> is structured, and how to think about a problem.**
>
> That's why we teach the foundation first and the role second. Choosing wrong
> costs you weeks of retraining, not years — as long as the floor underneath is
> solid.
