# Abstract — Job Seekers Guide

This folder is the **thinking layer** of the project. Not code, not tickets — the
_why_, the _who_, and the _shape_ of the product. Anything built in
`wp-content/` should be traceable back to a document here.

## One-line summary

> Everything a training institute charges ₹80,000 for already exists free on the
> internet. What doesn't exist free is **the order, the filter, and the
> accountability**. That is the product.
