# Persona 5 — The Role-Targeted Learner

**Shorthand:** Sana, 24, has decided: "I want to be a data engineer."

## Situation

Has already picked a destination — data engineer, web developer, DevOps,
QA automation, Salesforce, mobile. Picked it from a YouTube video, a salary
screenshot, or a friend's job title. What she does not have is the route, or any
way to check whether the destination is right for her.

## What they have

- Decisiveness and a target to aim at
- Usually a bookmarked roadmap image she has never executed

## What they lack

- Honest information about what that role actually does daily
- Awareness that the role sits on the same foundations as every other role
- Sequence — she wants to start at Spark and skip everything under it

## What they believe (wrongly)

- "I can skip DSA/OS/networks and go straight to the tools." — this is the
  single most common and most costly mistake
- "The roadmap image is the plan." — it's a list, not a plan
- "Certifications get jobs." — projects do

## Fears

That she picked the wrong role. That the market for it will close. That she's
already late.

## What the platform does for them

- Does **not** let her skip Module 0 — but reframes it: "here's what a data
  engineer actually does on a Tuesday; still want it?"
- Shows honestly which foundations her chosen role leans on hardest
  (data engineer → DBMS, OS, networks, distributed systems; frontend → web,
  OOP, a little DSA; DevOps → OS, networks, scripting)
- Recommends the language that fits the role (Python for data, TypeScript for
  frontend, Java for enterprise backend)
- Lets her keep her target while enforcing the base underneath it
- Track-specific projects instead of generic ones

## Assembled path

`How IT Works → Role Reality Check → Foundations (role-weighted) → Role's
language → Role track courses → Role projects → Resume → Job Search →
Interviews → Offer & Switch`

## Success looks like

Either landing the role she named, or discovering in week two that she actually
wants a different one — before spending a year on it.
