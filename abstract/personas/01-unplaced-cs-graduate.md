# Persona 1 — The Unplaced CS Graduate

**Shorthand:** Aarav, 22, B.E. Computer Science, tier-3 college, Nagpur

## Situation

Graduated with a CS/IT degree. Cleared every semester. Was not placed on campus,
or was placed in a service company that deferred the joining date indefinitely.
Six months out, still at home, still "preparing."

## What they have

- Familiar with the vocabulary — knows what a database *is*, has heard of OOP
- Wrote code for lab exams and copied a final-year project
- A resume with "Java, Python, C, HTML, CSS, MySQL" listed as skills

## What they lack

- Ability to *do* anything unaided — theory memorised, never applied
- One language they are genuinely fluent in
- Any project they can explain line by line
- A resume that survives an ATS filter
- Any idea why they keep failing round 2

## What they believe (wrongly)

- "My college tier is the reason." — partly, but it is not the binding constraint
- "I need to learn 5 more technologies." — no, you need to finish one
- "DSA is the whole game." — it's one round of several
- "I studied all this in college, so I know it." — you passed an exam on it

## Fears

Being overtaken by juniors. Explaining the gap year at home. Wasting money on
another course. That the window has already closed.

## What the platform does for them

- Starts at **Level 2 — "I know a little"**, skipping absolute basics
- Foundation subjects retaught **application-first**: not "define deadlock" but
  "here's a deadlock, break it"
- Forces one language to depth (Java recommended)
- Projects 1–4, each defensible in an interview
- Full job-search module, with heavy weight on the resume rewrite and the
  post-mortem habit

## Assembled path

`How IT Works → Foundations (all 9) → Java (deep) → SQL/DBMS applied → Web →
Projects 1–4 → Resume → Job Search → Interviews → Offer & Switch`

## Success looks like

An interview where they explain their own project without notes, and a rejection
they can name the reason for.
