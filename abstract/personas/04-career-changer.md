# Persona 4 — The Career Changer

**Shorthand:** Dr. Meera, 29, BDS / teacher / banker / civil engineer / lawyer,
3–7 years in a non-tech career

## Situation

Established in a field she no longer wants — long hours, low ceiling, wrong fit,
or an industry in decline. She has decided to move into IT. She is not starting
from ignorance; she is starting from a *different* expertise.

## What they have

- Professional maturity, communication, stakeholder skills — genuinely rare
  among freshers and genuinely valued
- Domain knowledge that can become a moat (health-tech, ed-tech, fintech,
  legal-tech)
- The ability to self-manage and finish things
- Usually some savings, so the constraint is time, not money

## What they lack

- All CS foundations
- Currency — no campus channel, no fresher tag, and age-anchored expectations
- A story that makes the switch sound deliberate rather than desperate

## What they believe (wrongly)

- "I'm too old." — the market cares about capability and cost, not birth year
- "I have to start at fresher salary." — often initially yes, but the domain
  premium is real and arrives fast
- "I should hide my past career." — the exact opposite

## Fears

The income dip. Explaining the switch. Competing against 22-year-olds with more
time and fewer obligations. Sunk cost in the old profession.

## What the platform does for them

- Starts at **Level 1**, but with **compressed pacing** — she reads fast and
  needs the *why* not the hand-holding
- Foundations trimmed to the load-bearing parts, since she has less runway
- Strong steer toward roles where domain knowledge compounds: business analyst,
  product, data, QA-to-automation, or full-stack in her old industry's vertical
- A dedicated lesson on **positioning the career change** — the switch narrative
  as an asset, mapping old-career skills onto new-career language
- Realistic timeline and money guidance stated up front, not sugar-coated

## Assembled path

`How IT Works → What Role Suits Me (domain-weighted) → Foundations (essential
subset) → One language (Python or Java by role) → SQL/Data → Web or Data track →
Projects 1–3 (domain-flavoured) → Career-Change Story lesson → Resume →
Job Search → Interviews → Offer & Switch`

## Success looks like

An offer where her old career is the reason she was chosen, not the thing she
had to explain away.
