# Persona 6 — The Early-Career Switcher (secondary)

**Shorthand:** Vikram, 24, 1–2 years into a low-paying service-company job

## Situation

He has the fresher tag. He is on a support desk, a manual testing seat, or a
bench, earning ₹2–4 LPA, doing work that isn't building the skills he needs. He
wants the *second* job — the one where the salary actually moves.

This was me at ₹13,000/month. See [../03-founder-journey.md](../03-founder-journey.md).

## What they have

- Real work experience, a company name, a payslip — enormous leverage
- Corporate vocabulary and process familiarity
- 2–3 hours a day, no more, and they are tired hours

## What they lack

- Depth — the job taught them a process, not engineering
- Knowledge of how experienced hiring differs from fresher hiring
- Any sense of what they are worth in the market
- Confidence to negotiate, or to leave

## What they believe (wrongly)

- "I should wait for the internal appraisal." — the switch pays multiples of it
- "Two years minimum before switching." — a norm, not a rule
- "My current company is my ceiling."

## What the platform does for them

- Starts at **Level 2/3**, foundations as targeted revision rather than
  first-teaching
- Emphasis on **System Design** and depth topics that experienced interviews
  actually probe
- Projects that demonstrate skills his day job does not
- The **Offer & Switch** module carries the weight here: market rate research,
  the experienced-hire resume, notice periods, counter-offers, negotiation, the
  graceful exit
- Time-realistic pacing built for someone with a full-time job

## Assembled path

`Foundations (revision) → Depth in current language → System Design →
Projects 2–4 → Experienced Resume → Job Search (referral-weighted) →
Interviews (experienced track) → Negotiation → Offer & Switch`

## Success looks like

A switch that multiplies the salary — and the knowledge that he can do it again
in two years.
