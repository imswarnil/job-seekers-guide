I do not know how you landed here.

Job Seekers Guide is for people like me. People with potential who are lost.
People who have the capability, and no guidance, and possibly no money, and a
family conversation waiting for them about a safer plan.

I was not talented. I was not passionate about software. I did not clear a single
written round in college. What I eventually got was a **sequence**: somebody
telling me what to learn, in what order, and how to talk about it. That
sequence cost ₹40,000 at an institute in Bangalore, plus a year I did not need to
lose.

That is what this platform is. The sequence, given away.
