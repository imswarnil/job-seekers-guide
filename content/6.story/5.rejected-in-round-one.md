Placements came. I was not really interested in a job — I was interested in the
channel — but I tried anyway, because everybody tries.

Java. Quantitative aptitude. Written English. Reasoning.

I failed badly. Not "close but not selected" badly. I could not clear the
**first written round**. Not once.

::illustration{name="resume" caption="Nine seconds. That is roughly how long a first-round filter looks at you."}
::

The one exception was Tech Mahindra. I got through. I kept getting through. I
reached the HR round.

I was rejected.

I remember exactly how that felt, and the word for it is loser. Not disappointed.
Not unlucky. Loser.

College ended. No job. My family had no plan and neither did I.
