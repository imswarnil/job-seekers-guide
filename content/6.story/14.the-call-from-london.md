Then a call from London. A company was looking for my skillset — in Budapest.

Would I be interested?

I said yes. I cracked it.

I am now in Europe, working as a Salesforce engineer.

::illustration{name="versioning" caption="Every branch here started from the same trunk: Java and SQL, learned properly, once."}
::

Here is the part I most want you to take:

**The foundation was the thing.** I started with Java and SQL. I may not be
writing much of either now — but how to think about a query, which S. N. Dixit
sir taught me, is what I use in Salesforce every week. The MVC architecture I
half-learned in a training institute in Bangalore is the architecture of
Salesforce development.

Nothing I learned at the bottom was wasted. It got upgraded. That is what a
foundation is for: not the syntax, but being able to learn the next thing faster
than you learned the last one.
