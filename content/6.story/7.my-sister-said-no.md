Then my sister stepped in.

She said: my brother is not going to live here in Mahroni. He is going to
Bangalore. He will study there and attend walk-ins.

That is the sentence this entire platform comes from. Somebody in the room
refused to accept the default on my behalf, at a point when I did not have the
standing to refuse it myself.

So I did the research and I made a decision I want to state exactly as I made it:

::compare{caption="Not a choice between them. A sequence."}
  :::compare-side{label="What I told myself I was giving up" verdict="neutral"}
  YouTube. The channel. The only thing I had enjoyed in four years.
  :::
  :::compare-side{label="What I actually decided" verdict="right"}
  Everything goes into getting a job. The passion is not cancelled, it is
  **queued** — once I have a salary, weekends are for the channel.
  :::
::

And then, before I left, I shot my first vlog.
