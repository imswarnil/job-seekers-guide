I started giving interviews. And one day I got one.

A startup. Web developer. I was told I was the one selected out of approximately
a thousand people.

::illustration{name="code" caption="What I was hired to do. Six months earlier I could not have done any of it."}
::

The package was **1.8 LPA**. Thirteen thousand rupees a month.

I was happy. I want to be honest about why that number made me happy: my sister
was earning fifteen thousand and sending me eight of it. Thirteen thousand meant
I could live on my own.

My sister was not happy. She always thought I deserved better and that I was
intelligent. She was right about the first part and I am still not sure about the
second, but that is what having someone in your corner looks like.
