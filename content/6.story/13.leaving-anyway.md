I was not happy at Cognizant. Micromanagement, and the way employees were
treated.

After seven months I decided to leave. Seven months. I want that on the record,
because the advice everybody gives is "stay two years, it looks bad otherwise".

I cracked **PwC — 21 LPA**. And then **Twilio — 32 LPA with stocks**.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-trending-up" color="success"}
₹13,000 a month → ₹32 LPA with stocks. It looks unreal written down. It looked
unreal to me while it was happening.
::

Twilio is where I learned what a product company culture actually is, which is a
different thing from what people tell you it is. I was doing analytics in
Salesforce, in Go-To-Market. I stayed three-plus years.
