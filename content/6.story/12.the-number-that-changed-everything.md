Three years later Durgesh switched jobs and got **21 LPA**.

I was on 5.5.

I remember the feeling exactly, and it was not envy. It was: *what am I doing?*
Same start, same walk-ins, same nothing. He had moved and I had not.

So I decided to change, and I want you to see what happened when somebody with my
background finally learned how to interview properly:

kept learning anyway, which turned out to matter.
