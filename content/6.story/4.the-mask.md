In the final year I found the thing I actually wanted.

I wanted to be a YouTuber. A creator. I started a channel. For the first time
since school there was something I would stay up for.

And I told nobody.

I got to a thousand subscribers hiding behind a mask, because I did not have the
courage to say out loud, in my family, in my town, that I wanted to work in a
creative field. You can want something very badly and still not be able to say
its name at dinner.

::note
I am not telling you to abandon the thing you love. Read what I did with it —
that is the whole point of this chapter.
::
