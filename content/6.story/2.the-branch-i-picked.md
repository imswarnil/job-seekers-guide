I took admission at LNCT Bhopal, in computer science.

I want to be precise about why, because it is the single most important fact in
this story. It was not passion. It was not aptitude. I had never written a line
of code.

My brother and my guardians told me that in CSE, getting a job is easy and the
market is good.

That was the entire basis of the decision that shaped the next ten years of my
life. I had no dreams. I had nothing.

::note{icon="i-lucide-info"}
I am not saying that was wrong. It turned out fine. I am saying that this is how
most people actually choose, and every curriculum is written as though they chose
out of love. That mismatch is where four years go.
::
