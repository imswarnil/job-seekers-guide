I completed college. I studied. I did not enjoy a single subject.

Operating systems. DBMS. Java. Data structures. The exact subjects I now know are
the whole game. I sat through all of them and understood almost none of it, and
nobody in that room ever connected one of them to a job I might one day do.

::illustration{name="foundations" caption="What these subjects actually are. Not what they felt like at the time."}
::

I passed. Passing and understanding are different things and the marksheet does
not have a column for it.
