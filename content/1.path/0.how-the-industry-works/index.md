## Why this module exists

Because you cannot choose a destination you cannot see.

There are N tools, N roles, N skills, and every roadmap on the internet
contradicts the last one. A beginner facing that picks randomly, or freezes.
Both waste a year.

The foundation is what actually decides your career — but knowing what *exists*
is what lets you choose which foundation to lean on. So: orientation first,
choice second, code third.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-alert-triangle" color="warning"}
This is the module self-taught developers most often skip, and it shows in
interviews. They can write a function but cannot explain what a sprint is, what
QA does, or why the product manager is asking them for an estimate.
::

## Who this is for

Everyone. It cannot be deselected. If you have a computer science degree you
will still meet things here you were never taught, because this is the part of
the industry that colleges do not have and cannot fake.

## What it is not

It is not a survey of frameworks and it will not tell you which technology is
"hot". By the end you will know what the jobs are, what the words mean, and what
kind of work you would rather be doing — which is exactly enough to make the
next decision well.
