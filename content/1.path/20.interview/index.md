It is built, it is live, and it is smart. The last thing between you and the job
is being able to say all of it out loud to somebody who is deciding whether to
pay you.

## Why this is a track and not an appendix

Because "I built a university management system" and "here is the admissions
funnel, here is why the fee query was slow, here is the join that was quietly
doubling my counts and how I found it" are two different candidates, and only one
of them gets an offer.

::real-life{title="The question that decides it" source="A hiring manager, after the fifth candidate that week"}
"Tell me about a bug you found." Everyone has a project. Almost nobody can
describe a real failure they diagnosed, in order, with the tool they used. The
ones who can are hired, because it is the only part of the conversation that
cannot be rehearsed from a blog post.
::
