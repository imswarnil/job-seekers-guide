Every figure on `/run` carries where it came from. Anything read out of your
source rather than measured at runtime is marked as such, and anything that
could not be measured honestly is left blank with the reason instead of being
estimated.

Change a `WHERE` and the funnel moves. It was never a picture of anything else.
