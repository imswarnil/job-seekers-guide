The old site was a WordPress install with a database, a login, and a plugin
stack. None of that was serving anybody who came here to learn.

What replaced it is a folder of markdown. That is not a technical footnote — it
is the reason a lesson can be corrected by anybody who spots a mistake, and the
reason nothing here can quietly stop being free.
