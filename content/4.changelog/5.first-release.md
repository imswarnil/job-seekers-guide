Version one is not "finished". It is the point at which the shape of the thing
stopped moving.

What is real: the reading surfaces, the routing, the design system and the
story. What is not: most of the curriculum. Three subjects are written out of
the eleven the path describes, and the pace section on the front page quotes
week ranges for subjects that do not exist yet. That is stated here rather than
discovered by somebody clicking into an empty week fifteen.

The next release is lessons, not features.
