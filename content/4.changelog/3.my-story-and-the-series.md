## Start here

`/path` is now `/start`, and the nav says **Start here**. The old URL still
works. "The path" was accurate and slightly cold; somebody frightened of the
whole thing needs to be told where to put their foot first.

## My story

There is now a [full account](/my-story) of how this platform came to exist:
average student, no plan, no guidance, a family that could not fund one, four
years of a degree I did not understand, and every rejection and salary figure
from a ₹13,000 first job to a Salesforce role in Europe.

It reads with a timeline down the side that tracks which chapter you are in — the
story gets worse before it gets better, and it is worth being able to see how
much of the climb is already behind you.

## A ten-episode series

The same story, [told properly](/series), in ten episodes with a Netflix-style
player. The scripts are up now; the videos follow. Every episode page is a real
page with the full narration on it, because the writing stands on its own and a
row of empty placeholders would say the opposite.

## A logo that means something

The old mark was three rising bars — a chart, meaning "progress", meaning
nothing. It could have belonged to an analytics dashboard. The new one is a
magnifying glass over a job listing, and it sweeps across and settles on the line
it was looking for.

The same idea animates on the home page: every opening is already on the screen
and always was. What moves is the glass.

## Everything else

Technologies now appear as proper tiles in their own brand colours — Java, SQL,
HTML, CSS, Git and the rest — rather than as words in a list. And there is a set
of animated illustrations for lessons to use: a resume being read for nine
seconds, a query returning rows, an interview where the answer is longer than the
question.
