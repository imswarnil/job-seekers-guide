There is one person behind this site, and you are writing to him rather than to
a ticketing system. That is the good news and the bad news: replies are real,
and they are not instant.

## Pick the route that fits

**A mistake in a lesson.** Wrong code, a broken link, an explanation that does
not hold up.
[Open an issue](https://github.com/imswarnil/job-seekers-guide/issues) or use
the "Report a mistake" link at the bottom of any lesson. This is the fastest
route to an actual fix, because it lands next to the file that is wrong.

**A question about the material.** Where to start, what to skip, whether
something applies to you.
[Ask it in the discussions](https://github.com/imswarnil/job-seekers-guide/discussions/new?category=q-a).
Asking in the open means the next person with the same question finds the
answer, and the good ones end up on the [questions page](/faq).

**A correction you want to make yourself.** Every page has an "Edit this page"
link that opens a pull request. Typos through to whole rewrites are welcome.

**Anything private.** Your situation, your circumstances, a privacy request, or
anything you do not want in public.
**swarnilsinghaicse@gmail.com**

**A conversation about your own path.** If you want an hour rather than a
paragraph, [book a call](https://topmate.io/swarnil). That is the one paid thing
associated with this site, and nothing on the path requires it.

## How long it takes

I write this around a full-time job, so expect days rather than hours. Issues
and discussions are usually quicker than email, because they are where I am
already looking.

If something is genuinely urgent, say so in the subject line.

## What I cannot help with

I say this plainly rather than leave you waiting for a reply that is not coming:

- I cannot refer you to a company or get you an interview.
- I cannot review your résumé for a specific application at scale, though the
  interview track covers how to write one.
- I cannot promise you a job, and I would not trust anybody who does. The
  [terms](/terms) say why.
