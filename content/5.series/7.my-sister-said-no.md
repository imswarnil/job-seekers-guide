## The narration

My sister stepped in.

She said: **my brother is not going to live here in Mahroni. He is going to
Bangalore. He will study there, and he will attend walk-ins.**

That is it. That is the sentence. Everything you are reading on this platform
comes out of it.

---

I want to sit on this for a moment, because it is the actual mechanism and people
skip past it looking for the tips.

I did not save myself with willpower. At that table I had no standing. I was the
one with no job, no plan, no money and no argument. Whatever I said would have
been received as a young man avoiding a sensible option.

It took somebody **else** in the room to refuse the default on my behalf. Somebody
who had credibility I did not have.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-users" color="primary"}
My sister was earning fifteen thousand rupees a month. She sent me eight of them.

Not as an investment with terms. As a sister, from a salary that could not really
spare it, so that her brother could go and find out what he might be.
::

---

Then I made a decision, and I want to state it exactly as I made it, because it
is the most-copied and most-mangled part of this whole story.

I did not give up the channel.

::compare{caption="This distinction is the whole thing. Get it wrong in either direction and you lose."}
  :::compare-side{label="Abandon it" verdict="wrong"}
  Kill the only thing you have ever actually wanted, in exchange for a job you do
  not have yet. You will resent the job and you will not be good at it.
  :::
  :::compare-side{label="Chase it now, with nothing" verdict="wrong"}
  Pursue it with no income, no runway, and a family that cannot fund you.
  Romantic in a film. In Mahroni in 2018 it ends in six months.
  :::
  :::compare-side{label="What I did" verdict="right"}
  Everything into getting a job. The passion is **queued**, not cancelled — once
  there is a salary, weekends are for the channel.
  :::
::

Queued. Not abandoned. There is a version of "follow your passion" that gets
people who cannot afford it very badly hurt, and there is a version of "be
practical" that quietly kills the only interesting thing about somebody. The
order matters and the order is: income first, then leverage.

---

And then, before I left, I shot my first vlog.

It is a small detail. I keep it in because it is the proof the plan was real. I
was not saying "later" and meaning "never". I was pressing pause with the tape
still running.

---

Then I got on a train to Bangalore with a degree I had not understood, no skills
I could name, eight thousand rupees a month from my sister, and a plan that
consisted entirely of the words "attend walk-ins".

It was, by an enormous margin, the best position I had been in for four years.
