## The narration

You know this conversation. If you are from a town like mine, you have either had
it or you are about to.

You are home. The degree is finished. The relatives have stopped asking about
results and started asking about placement. And eventually somebody says it
directly:

*So — what next?*

---

My father had an answer ready, and it was a reasonable one.

**BTC.** Become a UP government teacher. My academic marks were good enough. It
is a secure job. It is a respectable thing to be in my town. There is a clear
exam, a clear route, and at the end of it a life that nobody in the family has to
worry about.

I want to be very careful here, because it is easy to tell this story as though
my father was the obstacle. He was not. He was doing the only rational thing
available to a parent with an unemployed graduate and no information: proposing
the one route he could actually see the whole of.

The problem was not that it was a bad plan.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-signpost" color="warning"}
The problem was that it was the **only** plan. Not the best of several. The only
one. And a decision between one option and nothing is not a decision.
::

---

Now the part that most career advice leaves out entirely.

My father could not spend money on this. We did not have it.

I want to say that flatly, without arranging it into something inspiring, because
almost everything written about "investing in yourself" is written by people for
whom that phrase describes a choice. For a lot of us it describes a thing that is
not available.

That fact was not a constraint on my options. It **was** my options. Every route
that started with "first, pay for" was already closed before I looked at it.

::note{icon="i-lucide-wallet"}
This is the single biggest reason this platform is free and stays free. Not
generosity. I know exactly what it is like to be filtered out of your own future
at the price step, and it has nothing to do with how capable you are.
::

---

And through all of it, I said nothing about YouTube.

Obviously. A thousand subscribers, in a room where the discussion is about a
secure teaching job in a family that cannot afford a wrong turn? You do not put
that on the table. You would not be argued with. You would be worried about,
which is worse.

So I sat there while the future got decided, holding the one thing I actually
wanted, silently, like something I had stolen.

---

That is where this story would have ended. That is where it does end for an
enormous number of people, and they are not less capable than me — they were in
the same room with the same one option and nobody in that room said anything
else.

Somebody in my room said something else.
