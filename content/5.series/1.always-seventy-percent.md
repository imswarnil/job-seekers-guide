## The narration

There is a kind of student nobody worries about.

Not the one at the top. Not the one at the bottom, either. That one gets called
in, gets spoken to, gets a plan. I am talking about the one in the middle. The
seventy percent. Every year, every subject, seventy percent.

Nobody sits you down about a seventy. It is fine. It is *fine*. And because it is
fine, you go through eleven years of school without a single adult ever asking
you what you are actually good at.

I was that student. My name is Swarnil, I am from Mahroni, and this is the part
of the story where nothing happens.

---

I took PCM. Physics, chemistry, maths.

I want to be honest about the reasoning, because there wasn't any. That is what
you take. If you have decent marks and you are not going to do commerce, you take
PCM, and then you go to Kota, and then you clear IIT or NIT, and then your life
is sorted. That is the sentence. Everybody in my town knew that sentence.

Nobody ever finished it. Nobody said: *and if you don't clear it?*

---

So I went to Kota.

Here is what I did not understand about Kota at seventeen, and what I want to say
very clearly to anybody sitting in that town right now:

**Kota is a filter. It is not a school.**

It is built so that most people do not clear. That is not a malfunction. That is
the design. Hundreds of thousands of students go, a few thousand get what they
came for, and the rest go home carrying a result they were structurally
guaranteed to get.

I tried. I genuinely tried.

I failed.

---

And here is the thing that actually cost me: not the failure. The conclusion.

I came home from Kota believing I had found out something about myself. That I
was not clever enough. That the people who cleared it had a thing I did not have,
and that whatever came next, I would be doing it as a slightly lesser version of
a person.

I was seventeen. I had failed one exam that most people fail.

And I let it decide what I thought I was worth for the next six years.

::note{icon="i-lucide-info"}
If you are reading this because you are in that exact position right now: the
exam measured how you did on the exam. It did not measure you. I know that sounds
like something people say. Watch the next nine episodes and then decide whether
I am saying it as a slogan or as evidence.
::
