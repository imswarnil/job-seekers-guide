I run this site myself. I am not a company, there is no analytics department,
and nothing here is designed to learn about you. This page says exactly what
happens anyway, including the parts I would rather not have to write.

## The short version

You can read this entire site without an account, without giving me anything,
and without me learning who you are. I never see your name, your email or your
progress. Two third parties can see something: Google, because ads pay for the
hosting, and YouTube, but only if you press play.

## What I collect

Nothing. There is no database, no account system, no analytics, no tracking
pixel and no server of mine that you talk to.

That is not modesty about scale. The whole site is a folder of markdown files
turned into static pages, so there is nowhere for your data to go even if I
wanted it.

## What your own browser stores

Your progress through the path is saved on your device, under the key
`guide:progress:v2`. It holds which lessons you have ticked off, which one you
were last reading, and two display preferences.

It never leaves your browser. I cannot read it, and there is no copy anywhere
else, which also means that clearing your browser data deletes it permanently
and I cannot restore it for you.

To remove it: clear site data for this domain in your browser settings, or open
the developer console and run `localStorage.clear()`.

## Advertising, and what Google sees

This site shows ads through Google AdSense. That is how the hosting is paid for
and how the path stays free.

Google is a separate company with its own rules, and when an ad loads it can:

- set or read cookies in your browser,
- use a device identifier and your approximate location,
- use what it already knows about you from other sites to choose which ad to show.

I do not control any of that, I never see the data, and I cannot switch it off
on your behalf. What I can tell you is where to switch it off yourself:

- **[Google Ads settings](https://myadcenter.google.com/)** turns off personalised
  advertising across everything Google runs.
- **[Google's own description](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)**
  of what it collects on sites that use its products.
- **[Your Online Choices](https://www.youronlinechoices.com/)** (EU/UK) and
  **[YourAdChoices](https://optout.aboutads.info/)** (US) opt you out across many
  advertising networks at once.

Ad blockers work on this site. Nothing is walled off behind one, nothing nags
you to disable it, and nothing about the course changes if you use one. I would
rather you read the lessons.

## Videos

The story episodes and any embedded video are served from
`youtube-nocookie.com`, which is YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode. The player is
not requested at all until you actually play a video, so simply having a page
open sends YouTube nothing.

Once you press play, you are talking to YouTube, and
[Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) applies to what
happens next.

## Running code

Most of the runnable examples execute inside your own browser and go nowhere.

Java is the exception. It needs a real compiler, so when you run a Java snippet
the code you typed is sent to a small server of mine at
`jsg-runner.jobseekersguide.workers.dev`, compiled, and the output sent back. The
code is not stored and is not attached to you. Do not paste anything private
into it, which is good advice for any online code runner.

## Hosting

The site is served by GitHub Pages. Like every web server on earth, GitHub's
receives your IP address and browser details in order to send you the page, and
it may keep those in its logs. That is GitHub's, not mine, and it is covered by
[GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).

## The newsletter

The sign-up box in the footer does not work yet. It posts nowhere and stores
nothing, deliberately, rather than quietly collecting addresses for a list that
does not exist.

If it is ever switched on it will use an email provider, that provider will hold
the address you give it, and this page will say which one before it takes a
single address.

## Sign-in

There are `/login` and `/signup` pages. They are scaffolding and do not work.
Nothing typed into them is sent anywhere or stored. The entire path works
without an account and is meant to.

## Children

This site is written for adults deciding what to do with their careers. It is
not aimed at children, and I do not knowingly collect anything from anybody.
Since I collect nothing from anybody at all, there is nothing to delete, but if
you are a parent with a concern please write to me.

## Your rights

Data protection law in India, the EU, the UK and elsewhere gives you rights to
see, correct and delete what a site holds about you.

I hold nothing about you, so there is nothing to hand over or erase. Your
progress is yours already and lives on your own machine. For anything Google
holds, use the links in the advertising section above, because that is the only
place a request can actually be actioned.

If you think I have something of yours, write to me and I will tell you the
truth about it.
