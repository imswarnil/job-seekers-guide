## Why this subject

Every bug you cannot explain by reading your own code is explained by this
subject. The server that fell over at 40 concurrent users, the request that hung
forever, the two updates that produced one result — all of them are operating
systems questions wearing an application costume.

It is also, alongside DBMS, the subject asked most consistently in technical
interviews at every kind of company.
