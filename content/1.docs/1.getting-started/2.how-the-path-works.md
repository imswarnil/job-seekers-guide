## The ten modules

| # | Module | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | How the IT Industry Works | 2 wk |
| 1 | Computer Science Foundations | 20 wk |
| 2 | Your Programming Language | 12 wk ∥ |
| 3 | Databases in Practice | 3 wk |
| 4 | Web Development | 6 wk |
| 5 | English & Communication | ∥ throughout |
| 6 | Projects 1 → 4 | 6 wk |
| 7 | Resume, Job Search & AI | 3 wk |
| 8 | Interviews — HR & Technical | 2 wk |
| 9 | Offer, Joining & First Switch | 1 wk |

`∥` means it runs in parallel with other modules, not after them.

## The semester model

Foundations are chunked into semesters so eleven subjects do not arrive as one
undifferentiated wall:

| Semester | Theme | Subjects |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0** | Orientation | How the IT Industry Works |
| **1** | The Machine | Operating Systems · Computer Networks |
| **2** | The Data | DBMS · SQL in practice |
| **3** | The Code | OOP · Data Structures & Algorithms |
| **4** | The Craft | Software Engineering · Web Technologies |
| **5** | The Scale | System Design · AI overview · Theory of Computation |
| **∥** | Throughout | English & Communication · your language |

## Pace

| Pace | Hours/week | Full path |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Relaxed | 6–8 | ~44 weeks |
| Regular | 12–15 | ~28 weeks |
| Intense | 25+ | ~16 weeks |

Pick one, change it whenever your life changes. It drives the estimated finish
date and nothing else — there is no penalty for slowing down, because the only
learner who fails here is the one who stops.

## Paths are data, not code

Every path is a sequence of existing courses, lessons and projects. Serving a new
kind of learner means composing a new path, not shipping a release. That is why
the whole curriculum lives as folders of markdown: the structure of the tree
*is* the structure of the path.
