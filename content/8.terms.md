This is a free site written by one person. These terms are short because there
is not much to argue about.

## What this is

An ordered set of lessons on how to get a first software job, written by
[Swarnil Singhai](/my-story) from having done it. It is a free resource, not a
course you have enrolled in, not a bootcamp, and not a service you have bought.

There is no fee, no bond, no enrolment and no contract between us.

## The one thing I will not promise

**Nothing here guarantees you a job.**

Not the whole path, not finishing every exercise, not the interview track. I
have no placement arrangement with any company, I cannot make anybody hire you,
and I will never tell you what percentage of readers got placed, because I have
no way to know it and neither does anybody else who claims a number.

What the path gives you is an order to learn things in and an honest account of
what the work is. The hiring market, your circumstances and your own effort
decide the rest, and two of those three are outside anybody's control.

If a site, an institute or a person tells you otherwise, and puts a percentage
on it, that is the single clearest sign to walk away. That is most of why this
site exists.

## Nothing here is advice about your life

The lessons are educational material. They are not career advice specific to
you, not financial advice, and not a recommendation to leave a job, decline a
place, take a loan or move city.

I do not know your finances, your family, your visa situation or your health.
Decisions of that size deserve somebody who does.

## The material is free, and stays free

Everything on this site is public and free to read. You may:

- read, study and use it, for any purpose, forever,
- print it, save it, and read it offline,
- share it, link to it, quote it and teach from it, as long as you say where it
  came from,
- correct it, by opening a
  [pull request](https://github.com/imswarnil/job-seekers-guide).

What I ask you not to do is repackage it and sell it, or present it as your own
work. People being sold what should have been free is the problem this site was
written against.

That is a plain-English statement of intent rather than a formal software
licence. If you need a legally exact answer for something you are building on,
write to me and ask.

## Accuracy, and getting it wrong

I write this carefully and I still get things wrong. Technology also moves, so
a lesson that was right in 2026 may be out of date by the time you read it.

The material is provided as it is, with no warranty. Check anything that
matters before you rely on it, and if you find a mistake, please
[report it](https://github.com/imswarnil/job-seekers-guide/issues) so the next
person does not hit it.

To the extent the law allows, I am not liable for losses arising from using
this site. Given it is free and I am one person, I hope that reads as fair
rather than as a lawyer talking.

## Running code

The site runs code you type. Do not paste anything private, and do not use it
for anything beyond learning. See the [privacy page](/privacy) for where Java
code is sent and why.

Trying to overload, break into, or abuse the runner is the one thing that could
get it taken away from everybody, so please do not.

## Ads

Ads pay for the hosting, which is what keeps the path free. I do not choose
which ones you see and I do not endorse them. An advertiser has never had any
say in what a lesson says, and never will.

If you see an ad here that is scammy or offensive, tell me and I will block the
category.

## Links out

I link to other people's sites when they explain something better than I can. I
do not control them and I am not responsible for what they say or do.
