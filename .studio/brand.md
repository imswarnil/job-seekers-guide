# Job Seekers Guide

## What it is

One ordered learning path from no experience to a first software job. Not a
catalogue of courses you browse and not documentation you search — a sequence,
with the next step always marked.

The argument the whole platform rests on: **getting a job in software is not a
talent problem, it is a navigation problem.** Content is abundant and free;
direction is scarce and expensive. A ₹80,000 institute sells sequence, filtering,
somebody telling you what to do next, and the accountability of having paid. The
first three of those cost nothing to give away.

## Who reads it

People from mechanical, civil, B.Sc., commerce and non-technical careers, plus
CS graduates who were never told what the roles actually are. Often anxious,
often short on time, frequently convinced they need to be good at maths. They do
not.

## Structure

Subjects live at the root of the site. `/java` is a subject, `/java/collections`
is a module inside it, `/java/collections/generics` is a lesson. The order of the
path is the numeric prefixes on the folders in `content/1.path/` and nothing
else — reordering the curriculum is renaming a directory.

The sections, in order: introduction, computer science, languages, the web,
tools, building the application, AI, interview preparation. A section is a
stretch of the same road,
so every subject inside one is next to its neighbours in folder order too — a
section that reaches down the tree makes `/start` number itself 8, 14, 9 and
stop being a route.

## Visual language

- **Indigo** carries everything structural — navigation, progress, primary
  actions. It is the colour of study, and using it everywhere structural is what
  makes the site feel like one continuous path.
- **Teal** is the accent, and it is the real break from every other course
  platform in the category.
- **Amber** is rationed to exactly one job: streaks, milestones, "this is real".
  If amber is everywhere it stops meaning anything.
- Borders do the structural work. Shadows stay subtle — a teaching interface
  that floats everything reads as a marketing site.
- Type is sized for long-form reading first. Lesson prose is the product.

## What this is not

Not a job board. Not a placement agency. Not an interview-question dump. Not a
maths-heavy CS degree. Not chasing every trending tool. Say so plainly wherever
it comes up — a large number of capable people ruled themselves out of software
for reasons that were never true.
